Set the right goal for the month

Ramadan is a month to hold, not a month to chase records. You have less time to eat, less sleep and a different daily rhythm, so aim to come out of Ramadan as strong as you went in, ready to push on after Eid.

  • Keep the same exercises you trained before Ramadan.
  • Keep the weights close to normal on your main lifts.
  • Cut the number of sets by about a third.
  • If a session feels flat, finish your main lift and go home. Turning up four times a week matters more than any single workout.

Split your protein across three meals

Two meals rarely cover a full protein target. Spreading it across suhoor, iftar and a smaller late meal after taraweeh makes it much easier. Here is one way to split common targets.

When to train

Before iftarTrain 45 to 60 minutes before sunset and eat straight after. Keep the session short.
After iftarEat a lighter iftar, wait about 90 minutes, then train. Have the late meal afterwards.
After taraweehSome people have the most energy here. Keep an eye on your sleep if you train late.

There is no single right answer. Pick the slot that fits your work, your family and taraweeh, and keep it for the month.

A simple Ramadan training week

Four lifting sessions, one walk and two rest days. The rest day lines up with a cook day, so you are not trying to train and batch cook on the same afternoon.

Judge your progress fairly

  • Weigh in on the same morning each week, at the same point in your routine.
  • Expect the scale to move around in the first week, then settle.
  • Track the top set on two main lifts, not every number in your log.

Ramadan training and protein, answered

Should I train before or after iftar?

Either works, so pick the slot you can stick to. Before iftar means a short session 45 to 60 minutes before sunset and food straight after. After iftar means a lighter iftar, about 90 minutes to settle, then the gym and the late meal after.

Do I need to change my protein target in Ramadan?

Most people keep the same target they use the rest of the year. The hard part is fitting it into the hours you can eat, so split it across suhoor, iftar and a late meal. The Ramadan 2027 plan in the pack averages 118g a day from those three meals.

Should I lift as heavy as usual?

Keep the same exercises and keep the weights close to normal, but cut the number of sets by about a third. Ramadan is a month to hold your strength, not to chase new records.

What is a good high protein suhoor?

Something you can make in advance. The three suhoor recipes in the pack each have at least 40g of protein: Cardamom Date Protein Oats, Masala Omelette Wraps and Chilli Cheese Egg Muffins.

When does suhoor end?

Suhoor ends at the start of Fajr. The time changes every day, so check your local mosque timetable.
