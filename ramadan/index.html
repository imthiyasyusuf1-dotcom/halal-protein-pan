Same recipes, new schedule

Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February, depending on the moon sighting. You do not need a new cookbook for the month. You need a new schedule: suhoor ready before Fajr, iftar ready at sunset and a smaller meal after taraweeh, without cooking every day.

These guides show how the 30 day plan in Halal High Protein Meal Prep works. One cook day every four days makes two mains and a suhoor batch, and the last two days run off the freezer.
