Creamy Chicken Tikka Pasta

Tikka spiced chicken folded through a creamy tomato sauce and pasta. A dependable favourite.

572kcal
56gProtein
64gCarbs
9gFat

Per servingMakes 410 min prep25 min cook

This recipe is free in full. It is one of the 30 in the pack.

Ingredients

  • 650g chicken breast, diced
  • 100g 0% Greek yoghurt
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • small thumb of ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 400g passata
  • 120g light cream cheese
  • 280g penne or rigatoni
  • 100g spinach
  • coriander, to finish

Spices

  • 1 tbsp tikka spice blend
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli
  • 1 tsp garam masala

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the yoghurt, half the spices, garlic, ginger and salt.
  2. Cook the pasta in salted water for 1 minute less than the packet says. Save a mug of pasta water, then drain.
  3. Meanwhile heat the oil in a large pan. Fry the chicken for 6 minutes until charred at the edges. Add the onion and cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add the remaining spices, then the passata. Simmer for 8 minutes. Stir in the cream cheese until smooth.
  5. Toss in the pasta, spinach and a splash of pasta water until glossy. Finish with coriander.
Make it your way

Top with sliced green chilli and a pinch of chaat masala.

Meal prep and storage

Fridge 3 days. Add a splash of water or milk before reheating. Freezing not recommended for the creamy sauce.
