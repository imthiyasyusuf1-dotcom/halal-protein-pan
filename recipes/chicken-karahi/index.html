Chicken Karahi

Tomato, ginger, green chilli and black pepper. Proper karahi, cooked fast in one wok.

533kcal
51gProtein
49gCarbs
15gFat

Per servingMakes 415 min prep30 min cook

This recipe is free in full. It is one of the 30 in the pack.

Ingredients

  • 450g chicken breast, diced
  • 350g boneless chicken thighs, diced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 500g vine tomatoes, chopped
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed
  • large thumb of ginger, half grated, half in thin strips
  • 4 green chillies, slit
  • 100g 0% Greek yoghurt
  • big handful of coriander
  • 200g basmati rice, or serve with roti

Spices

  • 1 tsp crushed chillies
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp coriander seeds (crushed)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • half tsp turmeric

Method

  1. If serving with rice, rinse it until the water runs clear, then bring it to the boil with 400ml water and a pinch of salt. Cover and cook on the lowest heat for 12 minutes, then rest for 5 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a karahi or wok. Sear the chicken with a pinch of salt for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the grated ginger and garlic for 1 minute, then the tomatoes. Cover and cook for 8 minutes until the tomatoes collapse.
  4. Uncover, mash the tomatoes into the chicken and add the spices. Cook on a high heat for 8 to 10 minutes until the oil separates.
  5. Stir in the yoghurt off the heat, then add the green chillies, ginger strips and coriander. Cook 2 more minutes.
Make it your way

Finish with an extra crack of black pepper and a squeeze of lemon.

Meal prep and storage

Fridge 3 days, freezer 3 months. Rice: cool within 1 hour, keep in the fridge for no more than 1 day, or freeze in portions straight away. Reheat once only, until steaming hot.
