Tandoori Chicken Traybake

Yoghurt marinated chicken roasted on sweet potato and peppers, with a cooling mint raita.

493kcal
55gProtein
54gCarbs
7gFat

Per servingMakes 415 min prep35 min cook

Ingredients

  • 700g chicken breast, cut into large chunks
  • 300g 0% Greek yoghurt (200g marinade, 100g raita)
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • small thumb of ginger, grated
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 800g sweet potatoes, cubed
  • 2 red onions, cut into wedges
  • 2 peppers, chunked
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • quarter of a cucumber, grated

Spices

  • 2 tbsp tandoori spice
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli
  • 1 tsp dried mint
